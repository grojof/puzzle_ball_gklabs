# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Readable fans and bumpers
A fan SHALL show a housing with spinning blades and visible currents of air rising through its whole column, so its
reach and its hover height read before the ball enters. A bumper SHALL look like a pinball bumper, with a bright cap
and a chrome ring, on a short stand when it is a post, and SHALL light up and swell briefly when it throws the ball.
Both SHALL be recognizable in every biome and follow the depth style of the other elements.

#### Scenario: A fan in the snowy peaks
- **WHEN** a fan appears over a pit in the snowy peaks
- **THEN** its rising currents show how far up the column reaches, and they stand out from the snow

#### Scenario: Hitting a bumper
- **WHEN** the ball touches a bumper
- **THEN** the bumper lights up and swells briefly

### Requirement: Readable pendulums and seesaws
A pendulum SHALL show its pivot, its arm and an iron mace, with its swing path faintly marked, so the stretch it sweeps
reads as danger. A seesaw SHALL show a plank on a visible pivot support, so it reads as something that tips. Both SHALL
be recognizable in every biome and follow the depth style of the other elements.

#### Scenario: A pendulum in the volcano
- **WHEN** a pendulum swings over a run in the volcano
- **THEN** its mace and its faint swing path stand out from the lava glow
