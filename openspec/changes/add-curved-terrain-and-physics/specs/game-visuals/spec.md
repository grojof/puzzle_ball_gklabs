# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Readable new physics
- Curved terrain SHALL be drawn with the biome's terrain, smooth and seamless with the floor runs around it.
- Trampolines SHALL look springy and bounce visibly when used.
- Conveyor belts SHALL show their direction with moving marks.
- Ice SHALL look glassy and pale, and mud dark and soft.
- The loop SHALL read as a track the ball rides inside.

Each SHALL be recognizable in every biome and follow the depth style of the other elements.

#### Scenario: A backward belt in the desert
- **WHEN** a backward conveyor belt appears in the desert dusk
- **THEN** its moving marks show that it runs backwards, and it stands out from the sand
